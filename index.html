What JobProof does

Photo proof

Before, during, and after photos tied to the job, phase, and time — saved on the phone even without service.

Job guide for crews

Field workers see the scope, hours, and materials for each job. Pricing stays with the office.

GPS time clock

Clock in and out only at the job location, so hours are accurate and easy to defend.

Receipts & costs

Snap a receipt and it lands on the right job and phase. Costs roll up automatically.

Departments

Owners set up departments like Mitigation, Rebuild, Contents, and Roofing to group work their way.

Cost to bid & CRs

See every job's cost against its bid, plus change requests, at a glance.

How it works

  1. Set up your company. Add departments, jobs, and your team with the right access for each role.
  2. Crews work the job. Photos, notes, receipts, and time go in with a few taps — online or off.
  3. The office stays current. Everything syncs to one job record with costs, proof, and billing ready.
