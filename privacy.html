Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026 · JobProof LLC, Sioux Falls, South Dakota

This policy explains what information the JobProof mobile app and website ("JobProof", "we", "us") collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. JobProof is a business tool for contractors. If your employer gave you a JobProof account, your employer's company ("your company") controls the job records you create, and we process them on its behalf.

Information we collect

TypeWhat it includesWhy we collect it
Account informationName, email address, phone number (optional), company name, and your roleCreate your account, sign you in, and control what you can see and do
Photos and videosJob photos and videos you take or upload in the app, with the time they were takenDocument work on each job for your company

We only collect location when you take one of the actions above. JobProof does not track your location in the background. You can turn off location or camera access in your phone's settings, though GPS clock-in and photo capture will not work without them.

How we use information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve JobProof's features
  • To sync your work between your phone and your company's office users
  • To secure accounts and prevent misuse
  • To respond to support requests and send service messages about your account

We do not sell personal information. We do not use your information for advertising, and the app contains no third-party ads.

How information is shared

  • Within your company. Job records are visible to people in your company based on the roles your company sets up.
  • Service providers. We use trusted providers to host data and run the service, including Supabase (database, file storage, and sign-in) and Vercel (website hosting). They may only process information to provide services to us.
  • Legal reasons. We may share information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users and the public.
  • Business transfers. If JobProof LLC is involved in a merger or sale, information may transfer as part of that deal under this policy.

Security

Information is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS and stored with access controls that limit each user to their own company's data. Photos may be stored on your phone until you have a connection, and are then uploaded. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Retention and deletion

We keep account information while your account is active. Job records belong to your company and are kept until your company deletes them or closes its account. You can ask us to delete your account at any time. See Delete your account. We delete account information within 30 days of a verified request, except where we must keep certain records for legal, tax, or security reasons.

Your choices and rights

You can view and update your account information in the app. You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by emailing us. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law, and we will honor them.

Children

JobProof is a business app for adults. It is not directed to children under 13 (or 16 where required), and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update the date above and notify users in the app or by email.
