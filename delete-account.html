Delete your JobProof account

JobProof by JobProof LLC

You can ask us to delete your JobProof account and the personal information tied to it at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Email jobproofllc@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my JobProof account".
  2. Include your name and company name so we can find your account.
  3. We will confirm the request and finish deletion within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account profile: name, email, phone number, and sign-in details
  • Your app settings and device sync data
  • Location records tied to your time entries

What may be kept

  • Job records such as photos, notes, receipts, and costs belong to your company. They stay with the company's account, with your name removed or replaced, unless the company asks us to delete them.
  • Records we must keep for legal, tax, or security reasons, for up to 7 years.

Company owners who want to close the whole company account and delete all of its data can request this at the same email address.
